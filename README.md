## Analytical Methodology

Projects generally follow a structured investigative analysis process:

1. Define the analytical problem
2. Identify relevant information and data sources
3. Evaluate source reliability and limitations
4. Collect and normalize information
5. Resolve entities and relationships
6. Analyze patterns, anomalies, and networks
7. Test alternative explanations
8. Assess findings and confidence
9. Identify intelligence gaps
10. Produce actionable analytical findings

## Data & Research Ethics

Projects use publicly available, synthetic, or appropriately licensed data.

Projects involving criminal or terrorist activity focus on publicly documented cases and analytical methodology. No private, confidential, or proprietary information is used.

Data sources, assumptions, limitations, and analytical methodologies are documented within each project.
